EVENT VENUE RENTAL AGREEMENT
The Granary Event Hall

This Event Venue Rental Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between The Granary Event Hall, LLC (“Venue”) and Calloway & Reed Consulting, LLC (“Client”) for a company retreat dinner on Saturday, October 18, from 5:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m. (the “Event”).

1. Rental and Payment
The rental fee is $3,600, which covers the main hall, tables, chairs and the venue’s standard lighting. Client pays a deposit of $1,200 when it signs and the balance fourteen (14) days before the Event. The fee will not change after signing.

2. Setup and Access
Client may enter the hall at 3:00 p.m. for setup. «H»Client’s caterer must be licensed and must remove all food, waste and equipment by midnight.«/H»

3. Cancellation
Client may cancel by written notice. If Client cancels more than ninety (90) days before the Event, Venue refunds the deposit in full. If Client cancels fewer than ninety (90) days but more than thirty (30) days before the Event, Venue keeps the deposit. If Client cancels within thirty (30) days of the Event, Client owes half of the rental fee. If Venue cancels for any reason other than a safety emergency, it refunds everything Client has paid and pays Client $500 toward the cost of finding another location.

4. Insurance and Damage
«H»Client will carry event liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 and give Venue a certificate before the Event.«/H» Client is responsible for damage to the hall caused by Client or its guests, up to the cost of repair. Venue is responsible for the condition of the building, its fire exits and its equipment.

5. Rules
Amplified music must end by 10:30 p.m. Open flames are not allowed. Venue may stop any activity that creates an immediate safety risk.

6. General
This Agreement is for the Event date only and does not renew. It may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

Signed by Venue and Client.
