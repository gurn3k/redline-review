OFFICE LEASE
Suite 210, Maple Street Professional Building

This Office Lease (the “Lease”) is made between Maple Street Properties, LLC (“Landlord”) and Westbrook Accounting Services, LLC (“Tenant”) for Suite 210 of the Maple Street Professional Building, approximately 900 rentable square feet (the “Premises”).

1. Term
The term is twelve (12) months, beginning on March 1 and ending on the last day of the following February. The term ends on that date without any notice from either party. If Tenant wishes to stay longer, the parties may sign a new lease or a written extension, and neither party is obliged to do so.

2. Rent and Deposit
Tenant pays rent of $1,850 per month on the first day of each month. The rent stays the same for the whole term. «H»Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,850, which Landlord will hold in a separate account and return within thirty (30) days after the term ends, less any amount needed to repair damage beyond ordinary wear.«/H»

3. Use and Services
Tenant may use the Premises for general office use. Landlord provides heat, air conditioning, water and janitorial service for the common areas on business days. «H»Tenant pays its own electricity, which is metered separately.«/H»

4. Repairs and Insurance
Landlord keeps the roof, structure and building systems in good repair. Tenant keeps the Premises clean and reports damage promptly. Each party will carry the insurance customary for its own interest in the building and its contents.

5. Default
If Tenant does not pay rent within ten (10) days after written notice, or does not fix another breach within thirty (30) days after written notice, Landlord may end the Lease. Landlord’s remedies apply equally to a breach by Landlord, and Tenant may end the Lease if Landlord does not fix a failure to provide services within thirty (30) days after written notice.

6. General
This Lease is governed by the laws of the state where the building stands. Any change must be in writing signed by both parties.

Signed by Landlord and Tenant.
