FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
Golden Crust Bakery Franchising, Inc.

This Franchise Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Golden Crust Bakery Franchising, Inc. ("Franchisor") and the entity named on the cover page ("Franchisee"). Franchisor grants Franchisee the right to operate one Golden Crust bakery at the Approved Location for a term of ten (10) years.

1. Fees
Franchisee pays an initial franchise fee of $39,500 on signing. Franchisee's owners and its manager must attend Franchisor's two-week training program before opening.
Franchisor may increase the Royalty and the Brand Fund Contribution at any time by amending the Operations Manual, and the increase applies from the date stated in the amendment.

2. Operations
Franchisee will operate the bakery under the standards in the Operations Manual, which Franchisor may revise. Franchisee must buy approved ingredients and equipment only from suppliers Franchisor designates.

3. Termination
Franchisor may terminate this Agreement immediately on notice for any default, whether or not it can be cured, including a failure to meet any sales quota Franchisor sets from time to time, and Franchisee has no right to terminate before the term ends.

4. After the Agreement Ends
For two (2) years after this Agreement ends, Franchisee and each Guarantor will not own, operate or work for any business that sells baked goods within twenty-five (25) miles of any Golden Crust bakery.

5. Risk and Disputes
Franchisee will indemnify and hold Franchisor and its affiliates harmless from any and all claims, losses and expenses of every kind arising out of the operation of the bakery, including claims alleging that Franchisor was negligent in its training or supervision.
Franchisee must bring any claim against Franchisor only in binding arbitration in Franchisor's home city and only on an individual basis, and waives any right to a jury trial or to participate in a class action.

6. Guaranty
Each owner of Franchisee must sign the Guaranty and, by doing so, becomes personally liable for every payment and performance obligation of Franchisee under this Agreement, without limit and for as long as any obligation remains outstanding.

Signed by Franchisor, Franchisee and each Guarantor.
