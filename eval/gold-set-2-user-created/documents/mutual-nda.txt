MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made between Aldridge Packaging, Inc. and Tandem Print Works, LLC (each a “Party”) so that the Parties can evaluate a possible supply arrangement for custom product packaging (the “Purpose”).

1. Confidential Information
“Confidential Information” means non-public information that one Party (the “Discloser”) gives the other Party (the “Recipient”) for the Purpose and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. It does not include information that is public through no fault of the Recipient, that the Recipient already knew, that the Recipient received from a third party without restriction, or that the Recipient developed on its own.

2. Obligations
The Recipient will use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, will protect it with the same care it uses for its own confidential information, and will share it only with employees and advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by similar duties. If the law requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, it will tell the Discloser first where the law allows, so the Discloser can seek a protective order.

3. Term
This Agreement covers disclosures made during the two (2) years after the date below. Each Party’s duties for a given item of Confidential Information last for three (3) years from the date it was disclosed. Either Party may end the exchange of information at any time by written notice, and the duties above continue for information already shared.

4. Return of Materials
On written request, the Recipient will return or destroy the Discloser’s Confidential Information, except for copies it must keep to meet a legal requirement or that sit in routine backups. Neither Party gives the other a license to any patent, trademark or other intellectual property by sharing information under this Agreement.

5. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. Neither Party may assign it without the other’s written consent. It is the whole agreement between the Parties on this subject and may be changed only in writing signed by both.

Signed by an authorized representative of each Party on the date below.
