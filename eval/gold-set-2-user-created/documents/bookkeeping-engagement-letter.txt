ENGAGEMENT LETTER
Larkin & Moss, CPAs

Dear Ms. Alvarez,

This letter sets out the terms on which Larkin & Moss, CPAs (“we”) will provide bookkeeping and tax preparation services to Alvarez Landscaping, LLC (“you”) for the calendar year.

1. What We Will Do
We will record your monthly transactions, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, prepare monthly financial statements, and prepare your federal and state business income tax returns. We will not audit your records or give legal advice.

2. What We Need From You
You will give us complete and accurate records by the fifteenth (15th) day of each month and tell us promptly about changes in your business. You remain responsible for your own tax positions and for approving the returns before we file them.

3. Fees
Our fees for the year are $950 per month for bookkeeping and $2,400 for the tax returns, which we will bill at the start of each quarter. These fees will not change during the year. Work outside this scope is billed at $145 per hour only if you agree to it beforehand in writing. Invoices are due within fifteen (15) days.

4. Confidentiality
We will keep your information confidential and use it only to perform these services. We will keep your records for at least seven (7) years and give you copies on request.

5. Ending the Engagement
Either of us may end this engagement at any time by thirty (30) days’ written notice. You will pay for the work done up to the end date, and we will give you your records and a summary of the work in progress. This engagement ends on December 31 unless we both agree in writing to a new letter for the next year.

If these terms are acceptable, please sign and return a copy of this letter.

Very truly yours,
Larkin & Moss, CPAs
