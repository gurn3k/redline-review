PAYROLL AND CLIENT SERVICES AGREEMENT
PayRight Workforce Solutions

This Payroll and Client Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between PayRight Workforce Solutions, Inc. (“PayRight”) and the business named on the Client Schedule (“Client”). PayRight will run Client’s payroll, file payroll taxes and administer benefits for Client’s employees.

1. Fees
Client pays PayRight a per-employee, per-pay-period fee plus a percentage of gross payroll. Client must submit payroll data to PayRight no later than two (2) business days before each pay date.
PayRight may increase its processing fees, add new service fees and change its percentage charge at any time by posting the change in the client portal, effective on the date of the posting.

2. Term
The initial term is twelve (12) months.
This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Client delivers written notice of non-renewal at least five (5) business days before the anniversary of the Effective Date.

3. Termination
PayRight may terminate this Agreement or stop processing payroll at any time, immediately and without cause, and Client remains responsible for all fees through the end of the then-current term.

4. Taxes and Employment Claims
Client will indemnify PayRight against any and all employment claims, tax liabilities, penalties and interest, whether or not caused by PayRight, and without any limit on amount.
PayRight’s total liability for any claim, including penalties from late or incorrect tax filings made by PayRight, is limited to the fees Client paid for the one (1) month before the claim.

5. Employee Information
PayRight may use, sell and disclose Client’s employee data, including Social Security numbers and bank details, to third parties for marketing and analytics purposes.

6. Disputes
All disputes will be resolved by conﬁdential binding arbitration, and Client waives any right to bring or take part in a class or representative action against PayRight.

Accepted by Client on the Client Schedule date.
