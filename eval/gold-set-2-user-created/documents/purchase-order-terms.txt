PURCHASE ORDER TERMS AND CONDITIONS
Order No. 4471, Ridgeline Cabinet Co.

These terms apply to the order of kitchen hardware placed by Ridgeline Cabinet Co. (“Buyer”) with Hartwell Hardware Distributors, Inc. (“Seller”). They cover this order only.

1. Goods and Price
Seller will deliver the goods listed on the order at the unit prices shown. The prices are fixed for this order and include packing. Delivery charges are shown as a separate line.

2. Delivery
Seller will deliver by the date on the order, to the delivery address on the order. If Seller expects to miss the date, it will tell Buyer promptly, and Buyer may either agree a new date or cancel the late part of the order without charge.

3. Inspection and Returns
Buyer will inspect the goods within ten (10) business days after delivery and tell Seller about any shortage or defect. Seller will replace or credit goods that do not match the order. Goods that match the order may be returned within thirty (30) days in unopened packaging, and Seller may charge a restocking fee of ten percent (10%).

4. Payment
Buyer will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving it. Seller may add interest at one percent (1%) per month to an undisputed amount that is more than thirty (30) days overdue. Buyer may withhold payment of an amount it disputes in good faith while the parties resolve it.

5. Warranty
Seller promises that the goods are new, free of defects in materials and workmanship, and fit for their ordinary use for twelve (12) months from delivery. Seller will repair or replace any goods that fail this warranty.

6. General
Either party may cancel the undelivered part of this order by written notice if the other party materially breaches these terms and does not fix the breach within fifteen (15) days of notice. These terms are the whole agreement for this order and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

Accepted by Seller on the order date.
