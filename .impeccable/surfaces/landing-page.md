## Scope

Persuade. Public, unauthenticated landing page — the first thing the reader named in PRD.md sees.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- **Audience:** a small business owner about to accept a document they cannot negotiate, deciding in minutes whether to trust this before they sign anything.
- **Job:** believe the mechanism is real, not marketing copy.
- **Action:** exactly one — try it on a document.
- **Proof:** the page must demonstrate, not describe, a contract turning into ranked flags each showing the exact sentence it came from. Real product name (Redline). No invented prices, customers, testimonials, or logos.
- **Constraints:** no verdict on whether to sign; no legal-advice framing; no claim of handling scanned or photographed documents; no document types outside what PRD.md serves (Incoming paper only). Footer disclaimer from the app shell's positioning copy applies here too.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every flag is a stamped, verified confirmation clipped to the exact sentence it names — never a floating claim. Refuses the category's dashboard-card-with-a-score default.

OWN-WORLD: The Wire Confirmation — a bank-wire / teleprinter transaction-confirmation ledger. Ground: ledger cream `#f2ede1`. Ink: confirmation green `#1f5c3f` (Clear), stamp red `#9b1c1c` (Dangerous — reserved, appears nowhere else on the page), amber-brown `#8a5a2b` (Unusual), charcoal `#242220` (body prose). Tabular monospace for citations, reference tags, and labels; a plain grotesk for readable prose. Perforated tear-lines, continuous-feed ledger rules, rubber-stamp ink texture on severity words.

STORY: The visitor sees their own contract's language turn into ranked, cited confirmations in real time; understands each flag is checkable against the sentence beside it; trusts the mechanism instead of a promise; clicks to try it on their own document.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed two columns. Left: a vertical "source tape" of real contract prose, feeding downward. Right: stamped confirmation slips punch out one at a time, each tethered by a perforated tear-line to its exact source line; the lead flag is enormous, filling real vertical space rather than sitting as a card among cards; severity ink stays reserved per tier. The primary action ("Try it on a document") is a stamp-styled button anchored in view at all times.

FORM: The Wire Confirmation, candidate 7 of the grounded direction list, assigned by the roll. Raised by SCALE (from the declined vertical-feed challenger), COMMITMENT (from the declined manifesto challenger), FOCUS RING (from the declined night-sea challenger), and RESERVED INK (from the declined arcade challenger). Seed key `970d5bb0`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment

The lead flag's confirmation slip punching into place, tethered to the exact sentence that earned it — the reader can trace the claim with their own eyes before reading a word of copy.
