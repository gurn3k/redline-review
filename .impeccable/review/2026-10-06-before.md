# Before-review, 2026-10-06

Screenshot review of the live site (production at `b944cd1`) before the design revamp. Captured signed out (landing, login) and signed in (home, library, red lines, one analyzed document) at 1440px desktop and 390px phone emulation. Screenshots are in `2026-10-06-before/`. Nothing was analyzed, saved or deleted while capturing.

Owner's verdict going in: the look is "a bit plain and scrambled."

## Why it reads as scrambled

1. **Too many type voices.** Three families (Space Grotesk headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono), with monospace used for contract text, every quote, every small label and every button. Small all-caps labels ("§ INTAKE", "§ SIGN IN", "PROPOSED EDIT", "EXAMPLE LANGUAGE TO PROPOSE", "SAMPLE CONTRACT — NOT A REAL FILING") appear on every surface.
2. **The landing sample contract is hard-wrapped and then re-wrapped**, so lines break mid-phrase ("twelve (12) / §4.1 months"). It's the first thing a visitor reads closely.
3. **Wandering dotted tethers** between clause and flag cross the gutter and read as noise.
4. **Mismatched flag cards on the landing page.** One very large lead card, four small ones; the right column runs about twice the contract's height, leaving the left column ending in empty space.
5. **Content widths jump** below the landing fold: narrow, wide, narrow, wide. No steady grid.

## Why it reads as plain

6. **Beige on beige.** Ground, cards and quote boxes are all one tan family. Only the red border carries contrast.
7. **Tilted "stamp" buttons** (rotated about 1°) read as a theme, not as a professional tool.

## The analysis view (the actual product)

8. **The landing page's mechanism is missing from the product.** The real document page shows the full source text in a box at the top, then flags in a column below, with no highlight or link between a flag and its sentence.
9. **No overview.** The library knows "4 Dangerous, 2 Unusual"; the document page never says it. It opens on a wall of source text and a one-paragraph summary.
10. **Heavy flag cards.** Monospace quote in a gray box, explanation paragraph, monospace counter-offer label, all on beige; six stacked run past 4,000px.
11. **Half-empty layout.** The questions panel occupies the right column at full height with almost nothing in it.

## Home, library, red lines

12. **No navigation.** The header holds only the logo and "Log out." Moving between upload, library and red lines means returning home or using "← Back to home."
13. **Narrow content on a wide screen.** Library and red lines use about half the width and leave the right side blank; the upload card is wide and mostly empty.
14. **Rough details.** Dates as "10.02" (ambiguous), a washed-out disabled "Extract and save" button, a raw upload timestamp ("10/2/2026, 6:22:43 PM").

## For the two audiences

15. **"Small business" never appears** on the landing page. The pitch ("the contract someone else wrote") is generic.
16. **No byline, GitHub link or method** anywhere, and every real screen sits behind sign-up, so a hiring manager can't see the product.
17. **The owner's library is full of test files** (`injection-test.txt`, `<img src=x onerror=…>name-test.txt`, `double-click-test.txt`). Fine for testing; not for a demo or screen share.

## What held up

- Phone layout: at 390px emulation nothing overflows horizontally; pages are long but intact.
- Copy is plain and unhedged, and the disclaimer footer is present on every signed-in screen.
- Library rows already summarize severity counts, which the new analysis view can reuse.
